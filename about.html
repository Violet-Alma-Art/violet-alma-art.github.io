About Me

Violet Sleigh is a mixed media artist and designer in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. She grew up in a family that encouraged creativity, and began exploring various art forms at a young age. She worked in theater costume design and construction for 5 years. Violet now focuses primarily on fiber arts, digital medical illustration, painting, and linoleum block printing. Her art is influenced and inspired by American folk art traditions, architecture, botanicals, and her travels in the US and abroad. Violet seeks to use sustainable and repurposed materials in her work whenever possible. She hopes that her art inspires viewers to appreciate the slow and purposeful process of human creation, and maybe make something too.
